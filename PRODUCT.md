# Edivo Vina — product truth

## What it is

The first underwater winery in the world. Plavac Mali from Janjina on the Pelješac
peninsula rests three months on land, then goes into clay amphorae — corked and sealed
with two layers of rubber — which are locked in wrought-iron cages inside a fishing boat
that sank off Žuljana thirty years ago. It stays there ~700 days at ~25 m and ~14 °C.

Founded by **Ivo Šegović**. First wine 2011, first submersions 2013–14.
Everything is Croatian and nothing travelled far: Plavac from Janjina, clay from
Petrinja, wrought iron from Sisak, pinewood cases from Varaždin.

## The mechanism, in one sentence

The sea, not a cellar, finishes the wine — and signs every vessel differently, because
each one surfaces wearing its own oysters and coralline.

## Who buys

Primary: **someone buying an object, for a gift or a moment** — a 50th, a retirement, a
client gift. The business is the €382 amphora and the €536 TRIS; those are not wine, they
are objects. Nobody buys them for tasting notes.
Secondary: a collector who wants the sea to have done something *measurable* — served by
the TRIS comparison.
Third: a Dubrovnik visitor, one hour away, who heard about it → the Visit route.
Fourth: trade and hotels → one line, not a section.

They must believe, in this order: (1) it is not a gimmick, (2) it is unrepeatable,
(3) it will land when it is opened.

## Approved and not to be touched

The dark hero, the water column (teal → navy → abyss), and the WebGL descent: a 3D
amphora falls from the hero to the footer, lands upright in a welded rebar cage on the
seabed, with seagrass and three shoals of fish. A depth gauge counts 0–25 m and 0–700
days against scroll. The client has approved all of this explicitly.

## Facts we do NOT have — never invent

- **Depth and duration disagree across sources**: 18–25 m (their own site), 20 m
  (Euronews), 14 m (one dive source); "700+ days" vs "18–24 months". The whole site
  stands on these numbers in large type. Using their own until the client confirms.
- Tasting prices and opening hours: the only public figures are pre-euro kuna.
- Bottle dimensions (H, Ø). The amphora is measured from the 3D model at 300 × 108 mm.
- Scuba diving tours **no longer exist** (client confirmed 2026-09-04). Removed from
  navigation, footer and press.
